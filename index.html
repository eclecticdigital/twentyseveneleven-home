Est. MMXXII

Twenty Seven Eleven

We started in 2022 with one stubborn idea: that clothes should be built around the person wearing them, not the other way around. Everything we make begins as a flat pattern and ends on a real body.

The studio works in small runs — corsetry, capes, co-ord sets and occasion dresses — so each piece gets the attention a one-off deserves. Fabrics are sourced in short lengths, hand-finished, and cut to order. Nothing is mass produced, and nothing leaves until it fits.
